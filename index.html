The philosophy

more to it.

“What you know determines what you can ask. What you can ask determines what you can know next.”

Hitopedesh is where Hitesh Ahuja follows that loop. From the boardroom to the built environment, from an ancient story to a modern dilemma, the inquiry begins wherever a familiar explanation feels incomplete.

Ideas are examined in their context, connected across disciplines and brought back to the decisions we make. Sometimes the result is an essay. Sometimes a framework. Sometimes a book.

About

Hitesh Ahuja

Hitesh Ahuja is an architect, entrepreneur, business leader and author with a polymathic approach to understanding the world. His curiosity moves across management, design, history, mythology, science and philosophy, exploring connections that disciplinary boundaries often conceal.

Hitopedesh is the home of that inquiry: reinterpreting wisdom, one idea at a time.

More about Hitesh
“What you know determines what you can ask. What you can ask determines what you can know next.”

This continuing relationship between learning and questioning lies at the heart of Hitesh’s outlook. He sees knowledge as something to connect, examine and put to work. An ancient story can illuminate a modern dilemma; a scientific principle can offer a fresh perspective on business; practical experience can challenge an elegant theory.

His philosophy gives curiosity its discipline: study deepens learning, learning develops wisdom, and wisdom informs action. In his words, “Ultimately, vigour is a function of rigour.”

An Indian School of Business alumnus with over 25 years across entrepreneurship, design, strategy and operations, he brings a practitioner’s perspective to these explorations.

His forthcoming book, Maxim(um) Management, begins with a question characteristic of that approach: are management maxims universal rules or contextual wisdom?
